Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This document explains how Noobietoria collects, uses, stores, and shares your information when you use our services. By creating an account or continuing to use the service, you fully agree to the data collection terms below. If you do not agree, you are not permitted to continue using the service.

1. Data We Collect

To operate the system smoothly and prevent fraud, we are required to collect the following information:

  • Information you provide: Email address, Username, Date of Birth (for age verification), and password (securely one-way hashed).
  • Automated Data: Static and dynamic IP addresses, browser User-Agent strings, hardware configuration information (when using the game client), timezone, and login history.
  • Activity Data: All in-game/guild chat messages, online duration, virtual item/currency transactions, gameplay history, and the UGC (worlds, items) you create.
  • OAuth 2.0 Data: If you log in via Google, Discord, or Roblox, we will receive your Display Name, Email, and unique ID from the respective provider.

2. How We Use Data (Non-negotiable)

We use your data for essential purposes (and you cannot opt-out of these features if you wish to use the service):

  • Authentication & Security: To verify your identity when logging in, prevent mass account creation (botting), or password cracking attempts. We monitor IPs to permanently ban devices engaged in hacking/cheating.
  • Game Operations: To store your inventory, character state, and ensure all your actions in the virtual world are synchronized.
  • Violation Investigations: The entire transaction and chat history can be accessed by the Moderation Team to resolve disputes, scams, or harassment reports. Chatting within the system is not a completely private space.

3. Information Sharing

We will NOT sell your personal data to third-party advertisers. However, your information may be shared in the following mandatory situations:

  • Legal Requirements: When ordered by a court, law enforcement agency, or when we believe sharing is necessary to comply with the law or protect Noobietoria's legal rights.
  • Service Providers: Limited sharing with server hosting providers (AWS/Google Cloud/Viettel Cloud) or automated email services, which are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Noobietoria strictly complies with child protection laws on the internet. If you are under 13 years old, you are ABSOLUTELY NOT PERMITTED to register an account under any circumstances. We do not accept any exceptions, even with the consent of a legal guardian. If the system or the Moderation Team discovers any account registered by a user under 13, that account and all associated data will be immediately and permanently deleted without prior notice, and no refunds of any kind will be issued.

5. Data Security

We apply industry-standard encryption (such as bcrypt for passwords, transmission over HTTPS/WSS) to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure on the Internet. We cannot guarantee or absolutely promise that your data will not be subject to unauthorized access. You provide data to us at your own risk.
